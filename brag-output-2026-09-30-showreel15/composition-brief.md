# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Wanterest

## Objective
Create a 15-second motion-design showreel / launch video for Wanterest.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape, 1920x1080, 30fps
- Duration: 15s

## Source Material
- Source: `Wanterest product_launch.pdf` (app.wanterest.com "Product UI redesign" board), `src/app/globals.css`, `assets/fonts`, `packages/brand/src/logo-mark.tsx`, `public/brands`
- Product name: Wanterest
- Strongest claim: "An instrument, not a dashboard."
- UI to recreate: the Home "Today" screen (sidebar, ink Market State panel, Strongest signals, Recommended next move, What changed, Automatic monitoring)
- Verbatim copy: "1,284 conversations read", "37 relevant", "Teams are actively looking for a simpler CRM-sync tool. Pricing pressure against HubSpot is the fastest-rising reason.", "Make “simpler than HubSpot” explicit on your homepage.", "Know the day demand moves, not the day you check.", "An instrument, not a dashboard."
- Fictional demo data (Auterim Flow, "Michel") comes from the board itself.

## Creative Direction
- Tone: cinematic, showreel precision; lime used only as signal
- Hook: 1,284 -> 37 -> 12 funnel slamming in on the beat
- Outro: W mark draws, "instrument, not a dashboard." with dashboard struck through

## Visual Identity
- Background #f7f6f1, ink #111110, accent #d7ff3d
- Display Archivo 800, numbers/card titles Sora 600, body Inter

## Scenes
1. Funnel hook 0-2.46s  2. Today 2.46-5.74  3. Strongest signals 5.74-9.29  4. Next move 9.29-12.56  5. Outro 12.56-15

## Audio
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (~110 BPM); cuts locked to beats 2.46 / 5.74 / 9.29 / 12.56
- Audio-reactive: bed RMS (pre-extracted with ffmpeg, 30fps) drives lime glows and the ink panel halo, subtly
- SFX: CC0 Kenney, low HF-risk; soft impacts on cuts, card slides on signals, mouse click on Review action, bell on logo
