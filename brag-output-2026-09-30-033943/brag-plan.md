# Brag Plan: Wanterest (showreel cut, 23s)

**What it is:** Wanterest finds real buying intent in public conversations (Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, X, GitHub) and tells a product team the one move to make, with the evidence attached.
**For:** builders, marketers and product teams who need the source behind every signal.
**Angle:** a motion-design showreel where the product is the instrument. No black backgrounds: the whole film lives on the hero's stone stage with its lime base glow. The references (Sprites.ai film: light canvas, depth-of-field, rack focus, floating UI; SuperX reel: kinetic type, beat-locked hits, motion blur) set the craft bar.
**Hook:** hundreds of out-of-focus conversations drift in depth; "Real demand." rises; on the music's drop (2.0s) "Found." slams in on a lime block and the intent phrases in every post light up.
**Highlights:** (1) scan: sources check in one by one, 1,284 conversations read -> 37 relevant -> 12 qualified, posts stream into the card; (2) the Today window rises from the lime glow and the three strongest signals lift out of it in 3D, the 91 "Switching" signal racks into focus; (3) the recommended next move, "Make 'simpler than HubSpot' explicit on your homepage.", clicked.
**Punchline:** "Know what buyers want next." + wordmark + Request access.
**Tone:** polished/cinematic showreel. Motion does the pacing; every line holds long enough to read.
**Identity:** stone #f3f2ec, cream #f7f6f1, ink #111110, lime #d7ff3d; Archivo 800 display, Sora 600 numbers/titles, Inter body. Hero radial lime glow.
**Audio:** happy-beats-business-moves vol-1 (120 BPM) from 14.02s so the section drop hits at 2.0s; synthesized whooshes, soft ticks, sub hits, run through a shared room reverb and mixed under the music.
**Data note:** Auterim Flow, the signal cards and all numbers come from Wanterest's own product-design board (demo data). Background post snippets are illustrative texture.
