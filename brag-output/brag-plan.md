# Brag Plan: Wanterest

## What is this app?
Wanterest is demand intelligence: it reads thousands of public conversations and surfaces the few where someone is actually ready to buy, with the evidence attached.

## The angle
The source is the "Product UI redesign" board, whose thesis is **"An instrument, not a dashboard."** The video is a motion-design showreel that treats the real UI as the instrument: numbers count, signals arrive, the one dark panel is "the answer", lime is the only signal. Every frame uses the board's own rules (ink, cream, hairlines, lime) and its real copy.

## Hook (0-2.5s)
Ink black. Three numbers slam in on the beat: **1,284 conversations read -> 37 relevant -> 12 qualified**. The funnel is the hook, a demand instrument narrowing noise to signal.

## Key moments
- Home "Today" screen builds: sidebar, market-state ink panel typing "Teams are actively looking for a simpler CRM-sync tool.", stats 12 / 5 / 4 of 5 counting up.
- Strongest signals arrive one by one: r/sales 88 (High intent), r/SaaS 91 (Switching), Hacker News 81 (Problem), with score pips and topic chips.
- Recommended next move: "Make 'simpler than HubSpot' explicit on your homepage." A cursor clicks Review action; What changed ticks (Pricing pressure up 31, Workflow simplicity up 12, Manual reconciliation down 8); the Mon-Sun monitoring timeline lights up.

## Outro / punchline
Lime wipe to ink. W mark draws in. **"An instrument, not a dashboard."** then **wanterest** wordmark. Hold.

## User flow worth showing
Scan finished -> read market state -> open strongest signal / take the recommended move -> turn on monitoring.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (with app-store cleanliness)
- Creative direction: motion-designer showreel; precise, kinetic, confident; the product is the star
- Interpretation: big type, hard beat-locked cuts, fast-in then hold, camera pushes through real UI, restraint: lime used only as signal.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 15s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f7f6f1 (cream), ink #111110
- Accent: #d7ff3d (lime)
- Text: #111110, muted #8c8c82
- Display font: Archivo 800 (titles), Sora 600 (card titles + every number)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: Home screen ink "Market state" panel + signal cards; W polyline mark

## Share copy (draft)
An instrument, not a dashboard. Wanterest reads 1,284 conversations so you only see the 12 that matter.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with rhythmic bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (~110 BPM, beat 0.545s)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, fade-in 0.3s, fade-out last 1.2s; low under final hit
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Beats 0.27, 0.82, 1.37, 1.90, 2.46, 3.01 ... Strong cues 15.82 / 18.01 / 18.55 exist beyond the 15s window, so cuts lock to grid beats 2.46, 5.74, 9.29, 12.56.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; RMS breathes the lime glow and ink-panel halo. No visualizer.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched, low HF risk
- Audio-coupled moments: number slams, card-by-card signal arrival, cursor click, logo bell
- Restraint rule: no sound on every text; skip during dense moments.
