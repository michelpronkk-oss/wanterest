# Wanterest — 20s launch film

**Angle:** The internet is full of buyers saying exactly what they want. Wanterest hears the one
sentence that matters, scores it, maps it, and turns it into your next move.
One sentence travels through the whole film: *"Has anyone moved to something simpler?"*

**Tone:** cinematic × polished. Showreel-grade camera moves (3D tilt-in, push-ins, whip pans,
mask wipes) inside Wanterest's own restrained palette: cream `#f7f6f1`, ink `#111110`, lime `#d7ff3d`
used only where the signal is. Archivo 800 display, Sora, Inter UI.

**Hook:** a dark field of drifting public conversations. A lime scan line sweeps, everything
blurs away except one quote, and the word *simpler* gets the app's lime highlight.

**Music:** bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12`, starting at 5.74s into the track so its
lift (8.74s) hits at 0:03.00, the logo reveal. Beat grid (~110 BPM) in video time:
0.26, 0.82, 1.35, 1.90, 2.45, **3.00**, 3.55, 4.09, 4.63, 5.19, **5.72**, 6.28 … **10.10** … **14.45** … **17.19** … 19.91.
Every scene cut lands on a beat.
